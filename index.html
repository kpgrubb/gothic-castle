Plague's End

You are a scout, put ashore below the town and sent up to the castle alone.

For a year the Hochmauer paid its dues and answered its letters. Then the letters stopped — no tithe, no muster, no word of any kind, to your lord or to anyone. He means to know why a whole kingdom has gone quiet.

The gate stood open when you came up the road. No one came down to meet you.

Go in. Find out what happened here, and write it down.
